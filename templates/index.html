Help Us Map History

We're building a structured archive of a university newspaper spanning over 70 years. Your task: look at a scanned page and draw boxes around articles, advertisements, photographs, and other content. Every contribution helps preserve and understand this collection.

Start Annotating

What You'll Do

  1. View a randomly selected scanned newspaper page
  2. Draw rectangles around distinct content regions (articles, ads, images, etc.)
  3. Classify each region and optionally enter its headline
  4. Enter the publication date if it's visible on the page
  5. Submit and move on to the next page

Each page is reviewed by multiple volunteers to ensure accuracy. No account needed — just click the button above and start helping!
